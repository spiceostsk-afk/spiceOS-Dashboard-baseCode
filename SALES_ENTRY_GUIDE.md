# How to enter sales for a previous day

For when the restaurant was busy, or the system was offline, and a day's
sales still need to go in.

---

## Getting there

1. Sign in and check the restaurant name at the top is the right one.
2. In the left menu, click **Inventory**.
3. Click **Sales Entry**.

---

## Choose how you want to enter it

There are two buttons at the top right.

**Day total** — for catching up a whole day. Enter each dish once with the
total number sold that day. Example: Chicken Biryani 24, Butter Naan 60.
This is the quicker option and is right for most cases.

**Individual order** — for recreating one bill at a time, with its own time,
table and customer name. Use this when the separate bills matter.

---

## Entering the sale

1. **Date sold** — pick the day the food was actually sold, for example
   1 September. Future dates are not accepted.

2. If you chose *Individual order*, also set the **Time**, the **Table** and
   the **Customer** name.

3. **Paid by** — cash, card, UPI or other.

4. Add the dishes:
   - Choose the dish from the list. Its current price is shown beside the name.
   - Type the **quantity** sold.
   - Leave **Unit price** empty to use the normal menu price. Only type a price
     if the customer was charged something different that day.
   - Click **Add another dish** for each further item.

5. Check the totals at the bottom — subtotal, tax and grand total.

6. Click **Record sale on <date>**.

A green message confirms how many dishes were recorded, the total, and that
stock for that day has been reduced. The form then clears, ready for the next
day or the next bill.

---

## What this does

The sale is recorded on the date you chose, not today. That means:

- The money appears in that day's sales reports and payments.
- The ingredients come out of that day's stock, using each dish's recipe.
- That day's closing stock, and the next day's opening stock, are both correct.

---

## Things to expect

**The next day's opening stock will change.** This is correct. Once a day's
sales are entered, that day's closing stock drops by what the dishes used, and
the following day opens from the new figure.

**Only dishes that have a recipe will reduce stock.** A dish with no recipe
still records the money, but no ingredients come out. If a material is not
dropping when you expect it to, check that dish under **Recipes**.

**Enter each day separately.** Pick the date, enter that day's dishes, save.
Then change the date and do the next day.

---

## If you make a mistake

The sale is saved as a real sale, so it shows in Order History and in the
reports.

To correct a single entry, contact whoever manages the system — a sale can be
removed and re-entered.

Do not simply re-enter the same day twice. That would double both the money and
the stock reduction.
